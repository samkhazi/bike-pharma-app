Business rules behind the flows

No sale without a billEvery part leaving the store is on an invoice.
Barcode onlyInvoice items are added by scanning or typing the barcode.
Wallet ≠ creditWallet is rewards earned; credit is what a mechanic owes.
Overdue blocks creditAny overdue invoice stops new credit until it is paid.
CX with Mech = customer priceThe mechanic who brought the customer is noted on the bill.
Warranty from bill dateDays are set per product.
5 km verified mechanicsIf none is near, show the Bike Pharma Bike Surgeon.
Staff logins from AdminOnly customers and mechanics can sign up.
Receive payment in one placeOnly from Mechanic outstanding.
Insurance and PUC never lapseReminders go out before the expiry date.
People choose notificationsCustomers and mechanics pick what they get; offers skip people who turned them off.
Discount limitsBilling 2% · Manager 10% · Admin above.
