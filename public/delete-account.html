How to delete your account

  1. In the app, open Account and tap Delete my account. On the web, use the box above.
  2. Check it is you with the OTP we send to your mobile number. If you are already logged in, this step is skipped.
  3. Tick the box and tap Delete my account. Your account is deleted at once.

No longer have that number? Email samkhazi@bikepharma.com or call +91 63614 44960 with the number and your name. We check it is your account, delete it within 7 days and tell you when it is done.

What we delete

  • Your login (mobile number) and name
  • Saved addresses and bikes, cart and wishlist
  • Wallet cashback and its history (any balance left is lost)
  • Support chat messages and notification choices
  • Insurance or PUC details you saved, and reviews you wrote

What we keep, and for how long

  • Bills (tax invoices), orders and warranty records of what you bought. Indian GST law requires the shop to keep them for 72 months after the year’s annual return; then we delete them. Only Bike Pharma staff can see them.
  • Backup copies of the deleted data are replaced within 30 days.

These are business accounts, so the shop closes them. Please call +91 63614 44960.
