Privacy policy

Bike Pharma app and bikepharma.com · Last updated 12 November 2026

Bike Pharma Automobiles is a two-wheeler spare parts store in Ranebennur, Karnataka. This policy explains what our app (Bike Pharma on Google Play, com.bikepharma.app) and our website bikepharma.com keep about you, why we keep it, who else sees it, and how you can delete it.

  • We keep only what we need to sell you parts, bill you and stand behind the warranty.
  • We never sell your data and we show no ads.
  • You can delete your account at any time, in the app or at bikepharma.com/delete-account.

What we keep

  • Mobile number and name. This is your login. Each time you log in we send a one-time password (OTP) to your number by SMS.
  • Delivery addresses you save. If you tap “Use current location”, your phone shares its location once to fill in the address; we keep only the address you save.
  • Your bikes in My Bikes (brand, model, year and, if you add it, the registration number), so the shop shows parts that fit.
  • Orders, bills (invoices), warranties and wallet cashback from the store and the app.
  • Cart, wishlist and notification choices.
  • Messages you send to the shop in support chat.
  • Insurance or PUC details, only if you choose to save them for your bike.
  • Mechanic accounts (workshops that buy from us): name, garage, area, mobile number and GSTIN from their bills, and the verification details our staff collect (ID proof, shop photo and shop location).
  • Technical data: the IP address and browser of each visit, in our server logs, to protect logins (for example, to stop repeated wrong OTPs). One cookie keeps you logged in for 30 days, and the app remembers your language and display choices on your phone. Our pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when a page opens. We use no advertising or analytics trackers.

Camera, photos and microphone

  • The app opens the camera only when you scan a code (a part number or a mechanic’s QR code) or take a photo to search for a part. These photos are used only to find the part or the mechanic, and we do not keep them.
  • The microphone is used only when you tap voice search. Your phone’s speech service (for example, Google) turns what you say into text, and the app uses only that text.

Why we use it

  • To log you in and keep your account safe.
  • To take, deliver and bill your orders, and to send invoices, order updates and warranty reminders.
  • To show parts that fit your bike, add wallet cashback and show offers. You can turn offer messages off.
  • To answer your messages and warranty claims.
  • To keep the sales records that Indian tax law requires.

Who else sees it

  • Bike Pharma staff, to serve you: your orders, bills and messages.
  • Our SMS provider: your mobile number and the OTP, to send the SMS.
  • Our payment provider: when you pay online, the payment goes through it. We never see or keep your card details or UPI PIN.
  • Delivery partners: the name, phone number and address on an order, to deliver it.
  • Mechanics: a mechanic gets your details only when you contact them.
  • Vehicle checks: when our staff look up your bike to find the right parts, its registration number is checked with a vehicle registration data service, or its chassis number with the bike maker’s parts website.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs our servers in India.
  • The law: government bodies or courts, when the law requires it.

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data: while your account is open.
  • Bills, orders and warranty records: as long as Indian tax law requires (GST: 72 months after the year’s annual return), then we delete them.
  • OTPs end after 5 minutes. A login cookie ends after 30 days or when you log out.
  • When you delete your account, everything else is deleted at once, and backup copies are replaced within 30 days.

Your choices

  • See and change your details in the app under Account.
  • Turn off offer and reminder messages in Account > Notification settings.
  • Delete your account at any time: in the app, Account > Delete my account, or at bikepharma.com/delete-account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or anything about this policy. We reply within 30 days.

Security

All traffic to the app and the website is encrypted (HTTPS). Customers log in with one-time passwords, and staff passwords are stored only in hashed form. Only Bike Pharma staff can open shop records.

Children

Bike Pharma is meant for adults. We do not knowingly keep data about children under 18. If you think a child has given us their details, contact us and we will delete them.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
